# SAM physical mask geometry

This experiment retained a negative result. It tested whether mask-derived geometry complements the frozen image-embedding baseline. Meta's original SAM ViT-B processed one central crop per training photo: 518 pixels at the existing target scale of 5 pixels/mm. Fixed filtering produced 44 geometric and quality proxies per photo. Their means and standard deviations added 88 features per physical sample to the 2,562 baseline features.

Repeated out-of-fold Earth Mover's Distance (EMD; lower is better) was **34.532963**, versus the frozen control's **34.457862**. The estimated reduction was **-0.075101**, with a paired physical-sample cluster 95% interval of **[-0.349943, 0.133595]** from 10,000 draws (seed 20261110). Both unchanged requirements fail: a reduction of at least 1 EMD and a lower bound above zero. No new test descriptors, submission CSV or competition entry was produced. Official submission **56566750, public EMD 60.79507**, is retained.

All 127 training photos, representing 24 physical samples, completed. The three-photo feasibility pilot took 2.769 seconds; the remaining 124 photos took 75.474 seconds, reusing the pilot checkpoint. Allocated GPU memory peaked at 2.706 GiB and process memory at 2.272 GiB. The fixed filter accepted 11,629 masks. Masks may overlap and are not verified particles or sieve measurements. Twenty reused fivefold partitions still represent 24 physical sample clusters, not 480 independent observations. Earlier selection and camera-domain shift remain limitations.
